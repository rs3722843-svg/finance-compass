Emergency Fund ki Ahmiyat

Ek Emergency Fund hona financial stability ke liye zaroori hai. Yeh aapko unexpected kharchon (jaise medical emergency ya naukri khatam hone) se bachata hai.

Emergency Fund kaise banayein?

  • Apne mahana kharchon ka hisab lagayein.
  • Kam se kam 3 se 6 mahine ka kharcha jama karein.
  • Chote-chote savings se shuru karein.
